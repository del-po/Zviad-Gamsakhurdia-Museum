The Record Remains Open.

The Digital Archive brings together documents, photographs, video and audio relating to Zviad Gamsakhurdia’s life, work and public activity, bringing different forms of historical evidence into one accessible collection. This central Archive page allows visitors to search across all materials at once, while separate dedicated pages for Documents, Photos, Video and Audio provide more advanced browsing, filtering and viewing tools tailored to each type of material.
