Before you submit

Context makes a contribution useful.

A short explanation of where a material came from, who created it, when it was produced and how you obtained it may be as important as the digital file itself.

    Submission principles

    Contribution should preserve both evidence and dignity.

    Historical value alone does not remove copyright, privacy, confidentiality or ethical obligations.

    PROVENANCE

    Say what you know—and what you do not.

    Uncertain dates, authorship or ownership should be identified as uncertain rather than presented as established fact.

    ATTRIBUTION

    Original custodians remain visible.

    The museum seeks to credit creators, archives, families, collectors and contributors whenever legally and ethically appropriate.

    PRIVACY

    Not every record should be immediately public.

    Sensitive family material, personal correspondence and testimony may require consent, restriction, redaction or delayed access.

    CORRECTION

    Evidence-based correction is welcome.

    A correction is strongest when it identifies the existing claim and provides a document, citation or clear explanation supporting the change.

    Common questions

    Before sending material

    Can I submit a material even if I do not own it? +

    You may alert the museum to a material or provide a reference link, but publication requires an appropriate legal basis and accurate attribution. Do not upload a private copy when you do not have permission to share it.

    Will every submitted item be published? +

    No. Materials are assessed for relevance, authenticity, provenance, rights, privacy, technical quality and the museum’s current editorial priorities.

    Can I request anonymity? +

    You may ask that your public identity be withheld. The project may still need confidential contact information to verify the submission and discuss rights or provenance.

    How should I submit a very large collection? +

    Use the form to describe the collection rather than uploading everything at once. Include approximate volume, formats, date range, ownership and current location so a structured transfer method can be discussed.
